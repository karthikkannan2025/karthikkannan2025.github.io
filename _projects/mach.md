**Flight Test Engineer · Huntington Beach, CA · October 2025–March 2026**

## From development to flight testing

At Mach Industries, I supported a UAS program that progressed from **concept to first flight in three months**. Within that broader team effort, I planned and led selected tests: defining flight plans, coordinating execution, and documenting discrepancies and lessons learned.

My work spanned ground test preparation, aircraft communications, onboard video, and post-test data analysis. It gave me experience moving between software, instrumentation, and physical flight hardware as the aircraft developed.

## Ground test infrastructure

I designed and operated propulsion and avionics ground test infrastructure, including **engine test stands, hot-fire and thermal-soak testing, thrust measurements, and vibration characterization**. The work required translating test objectives into practical setups and interpreting the resulting measurements.

## Aircraft data and troubleshooting

I supported testing of aircraft communications and onboard camera streaming, as well as accelerometer data streams used in **GPS-denied navigation development**. I performed post-test data reduction and root cause analysis to help the team understand discrepancies and guide subsequent work.

## Communicating technical work

I delivered **technical and product briefings to senior government officials, military leaders, and international delegations**, adapting engineering information to audiences with different technical backgrounds. I also interviewed prospective engineers to support team staffing.

## What I took from the role

Rapid development made clear test objectives, usable data, and honest documentation of mistakes essential. Planning a test was only part of the responsibility; following through on its findings was equally important.
