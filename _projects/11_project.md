## The idea

I am exploring a **drone for wind turbine blade cleaning** as a personal robotics project. The concept brings together aircraft design, a cleaning payload, and the challenge of positioning a vehicle near a surface.

## Current stage

This project is in **concept development and design**. It is an opportunity to develop my mechanical, electrical, software, and systems engineering skills; a flight-tested cleaning system has not yet been demonstrated.

## Questions guiding the design

- How should the aircraft and cleaning mechanism be integrated?
- What tradeoffs connect payload mass, power requirements, and useful operating time?
- How could the system maintain suitable positioning and surface interaction during cleaning?
- What bench and integration tests would be needed before attempting a full demonstration?

These questions define the work ahead. They do not represent finalized hardware selections or demonstrated autonomous capabilities.

## Learning goals

Develop a more complete understanding of robotic systems by working across mechanical design, electrical systems, software, and integration. Future updates will document the concept choices and any prototypes or tests as they are completed.
