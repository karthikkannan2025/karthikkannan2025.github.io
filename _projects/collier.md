**Structural/Software Engineer · Raleigh, NC · May 2024–October 2025**

## Structural engineering supported by software

At Collier Aerospace, I analyzed and sized **composite and metallic aerospace structures using HyperX**. I supported design trade studies and technical reports for customers including NASA, Electra, ULA, Blue Origin, and Boeing.

I also performed **finite element analysis in FEMAP and NASTRAN** to evaluate load paths, stress distributions, and potential failure modes, and supported environmental loads analysis and high-temperature composites research.

## Translating structural mechanics into Python

Much of my software work used **Python**. I translated methods from structural mechanics references into tools that supported engineering analysis and design comparisons.

One application was evaluating **aircraft-specific spar cutout sizes and shapes**. Removing material can reduce weight, but the geometry needs to be considered alongside structural performance. I implemented analysis methods to support those trade studies across design alternatives rather than treating cutouts as arbitrary geometry.

## Automation and technical documentation

I developed automation scripts and plug-ins to streamline engineering workflows, and prepared analysis reports tailored to customer requirements. This work connected engineering calculations, software tools, and the documentation needed to review a design.
